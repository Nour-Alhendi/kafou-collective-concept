# KAFOU Collective Concept

Build a one-page website concept/prototype for "KAFOU COLECTIVO" — a 
multidisciplinary arts collective bridging art, music, and cinema. This 
is a discussion reference for a design meeting today, not a final product 
— focus on structure, mood, and layout.

MISSION & TONE:
KAFOU means "well done," "capable," "more than enough" (from Arabic). 
The collective is a representative bridge between art, music & cinema, 
and a space for artists and musicians to be seen, supported, and 
connected. Concept: "Uplift without hesitation. No gatekeeping, no 
competition — just pure creative power." Also offers workshops led by 
collective artists. Tone: raw, authentic, underground, community-driven, 
confident — not corporate, not overly polished.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
- Dark, near-black background with atmospheric smoky/hazy pink-magenta 
  gradients and fog/haze textures layered behind key sections (like a 
  moody concert or club atmosphere) — not flat black, but layered with 
  depth and mood
- Hot pink/magenta (#E91E8C or similar vibrant pink) as the dominant 
  glowing accent color against the dark, smoky background
- Typography: bold, blocky, confident sans-serif letterforms (uppercase, 
  strong weight) — clean and controlled, NOT scratchy/grunge/rock-poster 
  style. Should feel like it pairs naturally with an Arabic calligraphy 
  wordmark
- Overall energy: raw, underground, zine/club culture — but typographically 
  disciplined and confident, not chaotic or messy

SECTIONS (in order):
1. Hero — bold "KAFOU COLECTIVO" wordmark, tagline "Uplift without 
   hesitation," short intro line about the mission, smoky pink/dark 
   atmospheric background
2. About/Mission — the meaning of KAFOU, the collective's values 
   (respect, skill, raw authenticity — bridging art, music & cinema, 
   no gatekeeping, no competition)
3. Upcoming Events — grid/list of events with dates and an "Anmeldung" 
   style RSVP/ticket signup button
4. Artists & Workshops — a grid of artist profile cards, each offering 
   a workshop. Use these names as sample/placeholder content: Layla, 
   Hasna, Maia, Zedan, Amir, Anna, Sham. Each card should show name, 
   a placeholder for their discipline/workshop topic, and a "Book 
   workshop" button
5. Archive — past events/work in a gallery grid, with a note that this 
   could later include an interactive map view of past happenings
6. Newsletter signup — simple email capture with bold CTA button
7. Footer — Instagram handle @kafou.colectivo, email 
   kafou.colectivo@proton.me, links to all social channels, QR code 
   placeholder

Keep it as a clean structural prototype — prioritize layout, sections, 
mood, and hierarchy over pixel-perfect polish. This is meant to spark 
discussion with a designer about direction, not serve as final design.
